Antigen-Stratified CAR-T QSP Model: AD-Guided Development

B. Ma
April 2026

Executive Summary

This report documents the AD-guided refinement of an antigen-stratified CAR-T QSP model for BCMA-directed therapy in multiple myeloma. The initial model, while structurally reasonable, had a numerical pathology (stiffness ratio 10¹², tumor half-life of microseconds) that rendered it unusable. Through six structural edits, each validated with ForwardDiff.jl Jacobian inspection, the model now reproduces three clinically distinct outcomes across antigen-heterogeneity scenarios with a stiffness ratio near unity.

Parameter sensitivity analysis identifies five dominant levers (Vmax_mono, EC50_mono, k_prolif, k_decay_mem, and the β/X0 exhaustion pair) and one structural identifiability issue (β and X0 are only identifiable as a ratio).

Model Structure

The model has 8 state variables:

  • E_eff: effector CAR-T (fast-decay, primary cytotoxic population)

  • T_high, T_mid, T_low: tumor strata by BCMA expression

  • sBCMA: soluble BCMA in plasma

  • LD: lymphodepletion niche availability

  • E_mem: memory CAR-T (slow-decay, long-term surveillance)

  • X: cumulative exhaustion burden (dimensionless)

The three outcomes are:

  • antigen-homogeneous (90% high, 10% mid, 0% low): complete remission

  • typical MM (60/30/10): deep remission with minimal residual disease

  • pretreated/heavy (30/30/40): treatment failure via exhaustion-driven escape

The pretreated/heavy result emerges because the large initial T_low burden sustains chronic low-saturation kill, accumulating exhaustion X above the escape threshold (~0.12 under current parameterization). Once X exceeds threshold, the kill-engagement-exhaustion feedback loop locks the system in a terminal state at carrying capacity.

Interpretation

Dominant levers (|sens| > 3):

  • Vmax_mono (-12.9) and EC50_mono (+12.6): the antigen-low kill Hill curve parameters. At A_low = 30 we operate in the linear Hill regime (well below EC50 = 200), so these parameters modulate kill rate nearly linearly.

  • k_prolif (+4.9): tumor doubling rate. Direct driver of the kill-vs-growth competition.

  • k_decay_mem (+3.3): memory CAR-T half-life. Determines the length of the surveillance window.

  • beta_exh (+3.85) and X0_exh (-3.85): these are exactly equal and opposite, indicating the model depends only on the ratio β/X0. These two parameters are not independently identifiable from outcome data.

  • k_exh_recov (-3.13): exhaustion recovery timescale.

Modulatory (|sens| ~ 1-2):

  • Km_E (+1.86), E_max (-1.75), frac_mem (-1.68), k_growth (-1.44)

Essentially irrelevant for T_low at day 365:

  • Vmax_bival (-0.81), k_decay_eff (-0.39), EC50_bival (+0.06), Kd_sBCMA (-0.003)

The bivalent kill parameters and the sBCMA decoy mechanism operate only during the first ~10 days, when Thigh and Tmid dominate. By day 365 their influence has washed out.

Structural identifiability: the β/X0 degeneracy

The equal-and-opposite sensitivities of beta_exh and X0_exh are a direct consequence of the exhaustion dynamics:

\[ \frac{dX}{dt} = \beta \cdot \text{sat}_{\text{raw}} \cdot \frac{T_{\text{total}}}{K_{\max}} - k_{\text{recov}} X \]

\[ \text{sat}_{\text{eff}} = \text{sat}_{\text{raw}} \cdot \exp(-X/X_0) \]

X scales linearly in β, and the kill attenuation depends only on X/X0. The composite β · (integrated engagement) / X0 is what determines outcome.

Practical implication: for parameter estimation from patient data, fix either β or X0 and fit the other. Alternatively, reparameterize with ρ = β/X0 as a single identifiable combination.

Closing Notes

This refinement trajectory illustrates a general pattern for QSP model development: Jacobian inspection via AD is the fastest diagnostic for catching structural issues (wrong stiffness, unphysical timescales) and for identifying which parameters genuinely affect outcomes. Every change in this report was backed by a specific Jacobian signature — eigenvalues, self-derivatives, or sensitivity gradients — rather than hand-waving.

Remaining work would include:

  • Reparameterization to resolve the β/X0 identifiability degeneracy

  • Global sensitivity (Sobol indices) to complement the local analysis

  • Fitting to patient data where available

  • Extension to multi-dose regimens and combination therapies
